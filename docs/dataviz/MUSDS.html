            <br><br>
            Teezo Touchdown, a formerly underground artist from Beaumont, TX—we share an area code: 409. This collaboration perfectly captures the feeling of being overwhelmed, overstimulated, and insecure. It shows how coping sometimes looks obscure and clown-like, yet remains rhythmic and calming.
            <br><br>
            It reminds me that I have a home—not just physically, but metaphorically and artistically.

    <section class="section" data-section>
        <h2 class="section-title">I'm Sorry Ms. Jackson — Breathing in Greatness</h2>
        <p class="section-text">
            The Jackson family—beyond speculation, I see the love of art and how trauma affects those fighting to express themselves fully. Both Janet and Michael became social outcasts, yet returned time and again, breaking generational curses: poverty, suppression, greed, racism.
            <br><br>
            Their resilience through speculation, familial trauma, and public scrutiny created people highly motivated and determined. These videos force us to ask: How can anyone determine who deserves what, or who a person is, based on images and personas?
        </p>

    const particlesContainer = document.getElementById('particles');

        const particle = document.createElement('div');
        particle.className = 'particle';
        particle.style.left = Math.random() * 100 + '%';
        particle.style.animationDuration = (Math.random() * 10 + 10) + 's';
        particle.style.animationDelay = Math.random() * 5 + 's';
        particlesContainer.appendChild(particle);
    }

    // Intersection Observer for scroll animations
    const observer = new IntersectionObserver((entries) => {
        entries.forEach(entry => {
            if (entry.isIntersecting) {
                entry.target.classList.add('visible');
            }
        });
    }, {
        threshold: 0.1
    });

    document.querySelectorAll('[data-section]').forEach(section => {
        observer.observe(section);
    });
</script>
