Roots and Grounding

This is a digital archive of a few of my favorite music videos/visual album projects. I am creating a list of the videos that I've revered the most this semester as an ode to the artist's talents and vulnerabilities, however unseen. And this page, however unseen, will honor them: as my favorites, as my ancestors, and As my future.

My dream is to creative direct music videos, or at least to be a multimedia content creator where I can experiment with both videography and sound engineering; to channel the energy of sound into video, through my heart and soul; and it starts with the roots, the pieces of media that have allowed me to grow and digest information as I do.

These videos helped me get through this semester, and remind me of how I was able to pull through because I am strong, talented, and independent -- just as the stars who have been brave enough to speak their truths through their own multimedia projects and campaigns.

Touchdown — 409 on the Scoreboard!!

This semester brought incredibly tumultuous times that caused me to lose my grounding. Teezo Touchdown is a formerly underground artist from Beaumont, TX — and we share an area code: 409. This collaboration and promottion for Adidas perfectly captures the feeling of being overwhelmed, overstimulated, and insecure. It shows how coping sometimes looks obscure and clown-like, yet remains rhythmic and calming.

It reminds me that I have a home — not just physically, but metaphorically and artistically - outside of Texas.

Scorebooooard!!!

I’m Sorry Ms. Jackson — Breathing in Greatness

Along with Teezo Touchdown, the Jackson family, while many speculate on the truths behind this family – I chose to see the love of the game – art – and to acknowledge how trauma can deeply affect someone as they fight to express themselves fully. Both Janet and Michael Jackson became social outcasts in their lifetimes, and each time, they were able to come back as they broke one generational curse after another – poverty, suppression, greed, racism, etc. – and while some may stipulate on the circumstances, I’m deeply inspired by their ability to persevere through difficult times, social outcasting/outcrying, and familial trauma.

These videos demonstrate (both in form and content) how resilience through speculation, trauma, and public scrutiny can create a person who is highly motivated, influential, and determined – and it forces us to ask ourselves: how may anyone determine who deserves what, or who a person is based on images and personas? And how may projection affect both the projector and the projectee?

Psalms in the Keys to My Life — Exclusive Access and Affirmations

Stevie, one of the Wonders of the World, continues to inspire me through difficult times. Prince once said that Songs in the Key of Life was all you needed to learn about music and life. These are the keys I’ve found as I’ve continued to search for and uncover difficult truths to life.

These videos serve as reminders of the things I appreciate about and affirm to myself. Music and video have provided liminal safe spaces when physical ones were non-existent; and as I begin to make space for myself in the world, these music videos hidden behind affirmations will serve as the drywall and support beams to the fixed presence of my Self.

F*ck it, We Ball — Perseverance and Inspiration

Sometimes, when it is really difficult to keep going and see the light at the end of the tunnel, you really just have to treat life like a game – like it’s basketball or poker. With no wins or losses, only penalties and fouls, this life is too valuable to not ball out, to choose to fall out, and to be too afraid to call out – out of fear.

So, when deadlines hit you like a semi-truck, time melts into a different dimension, and the end and beginning of the self become blurry – you really just gotta get your head in the game and keep it there for as ling as possible, even when the game is played beneath the sand dunes of Father Time.

These videos range from live to recorded performances to capture how time affects consumption experiences.
